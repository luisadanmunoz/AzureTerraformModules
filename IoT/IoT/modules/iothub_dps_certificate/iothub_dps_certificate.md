# Módulo Terraform: IoT Hub DPS Certificate

Despliega un `Certificate` dentro de un `Device Provisioning Service`.

## Recursos Terraform

- `azurerm_iothub_dps_certificate`

## Entradas relevantes

- `iothub_dps_certificate_name`
- `resource_group_name`
- `iothub_dps_name`
- `certificate_content`

## Outputs principales

- `id`
- `name`
